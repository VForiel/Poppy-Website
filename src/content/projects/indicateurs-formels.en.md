## Why Non-Verbal Signs Carry Formal Diagnostic Value

Unlike older adolescents or adults, preschool children enact and somatize anxiety in distinct, observable patterns. Certain behaviors cannot be chalked up to developmental defiance or temper tantrums; they represent genuine **clinical signatures of early trauma**:

* **Stereotypical Post-Traumatic Play:** Mechanical, joyless repetition of an anxious scenario (collisions, attacks, drops) without narrative resolution.
* **Brief Dissociative Episodes:** Sudden zoning out, emotional detachment triggered by sensory reminders, transient selective mutism.
* **Motor Hyper-Vigilance:** Inability to engage in autonomous explorative play; continual surveillance of doorways and adult facial expressions.
* **Abrupt Developmental Regression:** Unexplained loss of acquired sphincter control, feeding regressions, or severe night terrors following specific events.

This initiative provides structured observational frameworks to give these signs diagnostic weight in child care and multidisciplinary settings.
