## An Imperative Clinical and Ethical Cause

In legal proceedings and child protective investigations, the testimony of a victimized child is frequently expected in explicit verbal narratives. When a 3-to-5-year-old cannot articulate events with chronological precision, their reactions risk being dismissed or discounted in judicial assessments.

However, modern developmental neuroscience and infant psychology unequivocally demonstrate that early somatic, behavioral, and relational trauma cues are **highly reliable, formal indicators**.

## Ongoing Actions

* **Interdisciplinary Cross-Talk:** Collaborating with family court judges, child attorneys, forensic child psychiatrists, and social care agencies.
* **Forensic Guidelines:** Developing observational benchmarks to ensure defensive silence or numbing in traumatized young children is not misinterpreted as an absence of trauma.
* **Public & Academic Dissemination:** Speaking at child welfare conferences and contributing to professional journals.
