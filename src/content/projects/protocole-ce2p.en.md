## At the Core of Lenval Children's Hospital

The CE2P department is a regional reference center on the French Riviera for comprehensive diagnostic evaluations in infant mental health and early childhood developmental psychiatry.

## Clinical Activities

* Administration of standardized psychometric and projective assessments (WPPSI-IV, WISC-V, Vineland-II, PEP-3, attachment scales).
* Joint clinical interviews alongside child psychiatrists, psychomotor therapists, and speech-language pathologists.
* Comprehensive assessments following acute adverse experiences and early relational trauma.
* Clinical report preparation for interdisciplinary care and school accommodation teams.
