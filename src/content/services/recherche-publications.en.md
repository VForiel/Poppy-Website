Bridging hospital frontline clinical practice and rigorous academic psychology research to transform our collective understanding of trauma in early childhood.

### Main Research Axes

* **Clinical Cohort Studies at Lenval CE2P:** Longitudinal follow-up of children aged 3 to 6 and validation of preverbal diagnostic markers.
* **Peer-Reviewed Publications:** Contributing articles to international journals in infant mental health, child psychiatry, and forensic psychopathology.
* **Specialized Reference Literature:** Ongoing manuscript preparation for practical manuals and monographs aimed at clinicians and protective services.
* **Symposium & Conference Presentations:** Sharing evidence-based findings across clinical and academic networks.
