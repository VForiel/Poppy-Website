Following years of clinical practice within the CE2P department at Lenval Children's Hospital, I am preparing the launch of my private clinical psychology practice in Nice.

### Practice Scope & Clinical Services

* **Early Intervention for Reactive Distress:** Single-incident or complex developmental trauma, early grief, conflictual separations, medical trauma.
* **Play-Based Psychotherapy & Clinical Media:** Safe, child-centered spaces tailored specifically for 3-to-6-year-old developmental needs.
* **Parental Guidance & Attachment Strengthening:** Supporting caregivers in decoding their young child's pre-verbal distress cues and re-establishing safe containment.
* **Full Psychological Assessments:** Socio-emotional and developmental evaluations.
