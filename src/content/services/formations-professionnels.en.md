As an active advocate for early clinical intervention in preschool years, I offer tailored training modules for institutions and professionals working in child protection, healthcare, and justice.

### Training Themes & Curriculum

* **"Decoding Psychotrauma Before Words":** Clinical identification of formal pre-verbal indicators in 3-to-6-year-olds who cannot yet formulate explicit verbal narratives.
* **Childhood Trauma & Judicial Systems:** Sensitizing judges, investigators, and child attorneys to non-verbal manifestations and trauma-induced behavioral symptoms.
* **Pediatric & Early Childhood Settings:** Early detection protocols within hospitals, nursery schools, and maternal-infant care centers (PMI).
* **Clinical Observation Scales:** Practical handling of validated diagnostic protocols to support forensic and clinical reports.
