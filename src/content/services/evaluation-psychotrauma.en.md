Assessing psychological trauma in preschool children (ages 3 to 6) represents a crucial clinical challenge. At this developmental stage, metacognitive abilities and verbal articulation of distress are still emerging, all too often leading to an under-recognition of lived traumatic events.

### Methodology & Clinical Approach

* **Systematic & Standardized Observation:** Identifying specific behavioral indicators (post-traumatic reenactment play, developmental regressions, hypervigilance, dissociation, somatic complaints).
* **Developmentally Tailored Mediations:** Utilizing symbolic play, drawing, and projective narrative scenarios to elicit emotional expression without verbal coercion.
* **Attachment Security Analysis:** Evaluating parent-child interaction dynamics and identifying reactive attachment patterns following early adversity.
* **Comprehensive Clinical Synthesis:** Delivering clear therapeutic roadmaps for families and healthcare multidisciplinary teams.
